Back in 2015 and 2016 I made three small Android apps: **RGB**, a color mixer, **Colorize**, a game about spotting the one square that's a different color, and **Transition**, a clock that slowly changes color. RGB ended up with over 100,000 downloads, which still surprises me.

Then life happened. The apps fell behind Google's requirements and were eventually pulled from the store.

This month I brought them back:

- **[RGB](/apps/rgb/)** got a cleaner one-screen layout and is live on Google Play again.
- **[Colorize](/apps/colorize/)** was rebuilt as a real game, with a grid that grows to 8×8, combos, ranks and a new Daily puzzle.
- **Transition** became **[Drift](/apps/drift/)**, an ambient clock with six faces, color moods, a night mode, and screen saver support.

All three also run right here in your browser, no install needed.

## What's next

I have a list of ideas I want to try, starting with a daily tracing game called Trace Race. I'll write about it here as it comes together.
