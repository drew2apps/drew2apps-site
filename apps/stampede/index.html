Survive 3:00

  • You're small and the munchers are big. You fit through 1-tile gaps; they don't.
  • Build walls right next to you, even on your own square: you get nudged out of the way. Make munchers take the long way. Wall them in and they chew through.
  • Towers shoot until they run out of acorns; walk past to reload, and a working mine close by keeps them topped up. Mines fill up; walk past to collect. They cave in once the ground is dug out. Fix and upgrade what you've built.
  • Fog hides the big map: munchers, gold veins, treasure chests and shrines. Survive four acts, three bosses and a final stampede.

Keyboard

WASD or arrows to move. Click near you to build. 1, 2, 3 pick; U fixes and upgrades; 4 or right-click knocks down. F tosses gold in a well. P pauses, M mutes.

Touch

Left thumb: drag on the left side to run. Right thumb: tap the big round button to drop a wall right behind you, or drag it to aim. The small buttons above it pick what you build.
