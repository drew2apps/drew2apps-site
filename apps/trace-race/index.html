How to play

  1. One shape a day. Everyone gets the same one, and a new one arrives at midnight. Monday is easy. By Sunday it's brutal.
  2. Watch the spark. During the countdown it shows where to start and which way to go.
  3. Trace it in one stroke. The clock starts when you touch and stops when you lift.
  4. Fast and accurate wins. Stay inside the lane and cover the whole shape. Accuracy counts the most.
  5. One try. Share your result, keep your streak going, and practice the shapes you've collected.
