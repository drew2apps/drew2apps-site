Privacy policy

Short version: the apps don't collect anything about you.

Effective October 14, 2026 · Applies to drew2apps.com and the RGB, Colorize, Drift (formerly Transition) and Trace Race apps on the web and Android, to Trace Race on Reddit, and to the Stampede test build.

What the apps collect

Nothing. RGB, Colorize, Drift and Trace Race don't have accounts, ads, analytics, tracking or third-party SDKs, and they don't send any data to a server. They work fully offline on Android. (Trace Race on Reddit and the Stampede test build work differently; see below.)

What stays on your device

A few settings are saved locally so the apps remember them: your saved colors and last mode in RGB, your high scores, sound setting and button-press count in Colorize, your clock face, colors and night mode settings in Drift, and your daily results, streak, practice best scores, sound settings and the path you traced in today's game in Trace Race. This information never leaves your device. Clearing the app's data (or your browser's site data) deletes it.

Permissions

The Android apps don't request any special permissions. Trace Race uses Android's standard vibration permission for haptic feedback, which you can turn off in its settings.

Trace Race on Reddit

The Reddit version of Trace Race runs inside Reddit, on Reddit's developer platform. To run each community's daily leaderboards and streaks, it saves your Reddit username and user ID with your results in that community: your score, time, accuracy and which shape you got each day, plus the path you traced, which is kept for four days so you can watch the replay. This data is stored on Reddit's servers. Other players can see your username and score on the leaderboard. It isn't sold, shared with anyone else or used for ads. If you tap "Comment your result", the app posts your result as a comment from your account, and only after you allow it. Sound and vibration settings and practice best scores stay on your device. Reddit's own privacy policy also applies when you use Reddit. For questions or requests about your Trace Race data, email drew@drew2apps.com.

Stampede (early test)

Stampede is a new game in early testing at drew2apps.com/apps/stampede/. To help balance it, each run is saved to a database on Cloudflare: game stats like how long you survived, the waves, your gold, what you built and where, the monsters on the board at a few moments, and whether you played on a touch screen. Runs don't include your name, an account, your IP address or anything else that identifies you. If you send feedback from the game, your star rating and comment are saved too, plus a name only if you type one. This data is used only to improve the game. It isn't sold or shared, and it will be deleted when testing ends. Your settings, and any name you type, are also kept in your browser so the game remembers them. To have your feedback removed, email drew@drew2apps.com.

This website

drew2apps.com is hosted on Cloudflare. Like any web host, Cloudflare processes basic technical information (such as IP address and browser type) to deliver pages and protect against abuse. This site doesn't set cookies. It uses Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page views; it doesn't use cookies or local storage, and it doesn't track you across sites or build a profile of you.

Children

The apps are suitable for all ages. The web and Android apps don't knowingly collect any information from anyone, including children. Trace Race on Reddit is only for people with a Reddit account, and Reddit requires users to be at least 13.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with a new effective date. Questions? Email drew@drew2apps.com.
